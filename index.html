ABOUT LIDU LETTERS

What is this site?

Lidu Letters is a simple place to read questions people have asked Lidu — and the answers she shared through a letterboard. You can peek at a few on the home page, or browse the full collection by topic, event, or date.

Who is Lidu?

Lidu (Liduvina) Burgoin is a nonspeaking autistic teenager who communicates by spelling on a letterboard. Her mother, Dalia Burgoin, has shared publicly that Lidu began communicating with her telepathically, and that letterboard spelling became a way to bring those messages into clear words.

Dalia has described Lidu as one of the nonspeaking autistic children in the same broader conversation popularized by The Telepathy Tapes — a podcast exploring reports of telepathy among nonspeaking autistic people.
